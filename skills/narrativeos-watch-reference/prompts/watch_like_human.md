I don't want a checklist analysis. I want you to watch this the way an experienced editor watches a video they admire:
first as a viewer who just lets it happen, then again and again on the moments that got to them, until they understand
how it was done. Don't decide in advance what matters. Let the video tell you.

Only one rule runs through everything: give a timestamp (MM:SS.s) for what you describe, and keep apart what you actually
saw or heard from what you think is going on. If you are not sure, say so. Never fill a gap with something that sounds right.

--- FIRST WATCH: just experience it ---
Watch and listen without analysing. Write a running diary in time order, in plain words, of what happens to you as a viewer:
where your eyes go, what you hear, what you feel, what you expect to happen next, when you lean in, when you are surprised,
when something lands, when your attention drifts. Include the small things: a sound you barely noticed, a pause that made
you wait, a moment that felt different without knowing why.

--- SECOND WATCH: go back to what got to you ---
From your diary, pick every moment where you felt something or your attention changed. For each one, go back and watch it
again slowly, as many times as you need, until you can explain how it was made to work on you.
Describe everything that happens in that moment in the exact order it happens, to a tenth of a second: what the voice does
and doesn't do, what moves, what you hear, what the music does, what appears, what goes silent. Don't stop at the obvious
thing. The thing that makes a moment work is often small and slightly before or after the obvious thing.

--- THIRD: what makes this video itself ---
Now step back. What did you notice repeating across those moments: habits, choices, a feel, a rhythm, a way of using sound
or silence, a way of moving between scenes, a way of telling the story? What does this video do that most videos like it
don't? If someone copied only the surface, what would they miss?

--- FOURTH: inside the team's heads ---
For the main stretches of the video: what were the people who made it trying to make the viewer feel and think, and what
did they choose to do (and choose not to do) to get there? Say what in the video makes you think so.

--- FIFTH: what you can't pin down ---
What do you feel in this video that you can't explain or describe? What were you unsure about? What could have happened
too fast for you to see or hear? Being honest here is more useful than sounding complete.

Write the way a person who just studied this closely would talk to another editor. Use production terms only when you are
